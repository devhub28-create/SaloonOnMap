# Reports & Business Analytics - SLOT Platform

## PostgreSQL Direct Aggregation
- All partner and admin reports utilize server-side SQL aggregation (`SUM`, `COUNT`, `AVG`, `GROUP BY`) rather than transferring raw datasets to frontend clients.

## Metrics Covered
- Gross revenue & completed revenue
- Booking volume, completion rate, cancellation rate
- Peak booking hours & top services/combos
- Staff and seat utilization rates
